# Sales Performance Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Sales Performance Dashboard** built using **Power BI** and the **Superstore Dataset**. The dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of sales performance, profitability, product categories, and regional contributions to support data-driven business decisions.

The objective of this project is to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights through effective data visualization and storytelling.

---

## Dataset

**Dataset Used:** Sample Superstore Dataset

The dataset contains information related to:

* Orders
* Sales
* Profit
* Quantity Sold
* Product Categories
* Customer Segments
* Regions
* Order Dates

## Dashboard Features

### KPI Summary Cards

* Total Sales
* Total Profit
* Total Quantity Sold
* Total Orders

### Sales Trend Analysis

* Year-wise sales performance
* Growth trend visualization

### Category Analysis

* Sales contribution by category

### Regional Analysis

* Sales comparison across regions
* Identification of top-performing and underperforming regions

### Interactive Filters

* Year-based slicer
* Dynamic dashboard interaction

---

## Key Insights

### Business Performance

* Generated over **$2.30 Million** in total sales.
* Earned approximately **$286K** in profit.
* Processed nearly **10,000 orders**.

### Sales Growth

* Sales increased significantly after 2015.
* Highest sales were recorded in 2017.

### Category Performance

* Technology contributed the highest share of total sales.
* Furniture and Office Supplies contributed nearly equal proportions.

### Regional Performance

* West region generated the highest sales.
* South region recorded the lowest sales performance.

---

## Dashboard Story

The dashboard follows a storytelling approach:

1. Understand overall business performance through KPIs.
2. Analyze sales growth over time.
3. Identify top-performing product categories.
4. Compare regional sales performance.
5. Discover opportunities for business improvement.

---

## Business Recommendations

* Expand investment in Technology products due to strong sales performance.
* Maintain growth strategies that contributed to the sales increase after 2015.
* Focus on improving sales performance in the South region.
* Monitor category-wise profitability to maximize business returns.

---

## Project Outcome

This project demonstrates how Power BI can be used to convert raw business data into actionable insights through effective visualization and storytelling. The dashboard helps stakeholders understand business performance, identify growth opportunities, and support strategic decision-making.
